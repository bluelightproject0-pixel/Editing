## rules (apply; candidates should be mentioned at checkpoint A)
- [R-before-any-full-render-first-build-or-re] (candidate, 0.99, global) Before any full render (first build or revision), show the user the key frames (hook, each new layout/graphic peak, ending) as stills and wait for approval; render only after OK.
- [R-split-screen-for-graphics-full-width-hal] (candidate, 0.95, global) Split screen for graphics = full-width halves (graphic top, speaker footage bottom), not a boxed card; the speaker's whole face stays visible.
- [R-chart-labels-never-cross-lines-markers-o] (candidate, 0.95, global) Chart labels never cross lines, markers or other labels at any moment, including zoom peaks; anchor zooms on the chart's bottom edge so content grows away from takeaways/captions; snapshot every zoom peak in QA.
- [R-keep-soundtrack] (default, 0.9, global) When editing existing footage keep its soundtrack (voice) and sync every edit to it; added music/SFX sit under it.
- [R-preserve-ending] (default, 0.9, global) Preserve the full ending of the source; extend after it (hold/CTA) rather than trimming it.
- [R-zoom-into-graphics] (default, 0.9, global) Include data/diagram graphics that build up, then move the camera into the key part (zoom-in), and zoom back out to the speaker.
- [R-before-delivering-measure-the-render-s-a] (candidate, 0.9, global) Before delivering, measure the render's audio level per second over the whole timeline; no unintended silence, voice audible from frame 0.
- [R-during-data-graphics-use-an-animated-spl] (candidate, 0.9, global) During graphics keep the speaker visible with an animated layout chosen per beat (split for data with reactions, PiP bubble for full-width visuals, overlay for keywords); captions sit just below the speaker's head in whatever layout is active.
- [R-captions-follow-the-speaker-always-direc] (candidate, 0.9, global) Captions follow the speaker: always directly below the head/chin, centered on the face, never over the face and never parked in a fixed band that ignores the framing.
- [R-after-every-render-backtest-the-file-reg] (candidate, 0.9, global) After every render, backtest the file: regress the final audio against each stem (voice, music, SFX) to confirm presence and level, measure per-second loudness, and snapshot key frames to confirm every visual layer; report the numbers before delivering.
- [R-before-every-checkpoint-run-an-independe] (candidate, 0.9, global) Before every checkpoint, run an independent high-effort critic (subagent) on dense frame strips of every transition and every text element; fix its findings before showing the user. Never present a pass you have not reviewed frame by frame.
- [R-ai-disclosure] (default, 0.8, global) Realistic AI-generated scenes get an 'AI info' disclosure note in post.md; never generate the speaker's likeness.
- [R-data-graphics-must-be-readable-each-grap] (candidate, 0.8, global) Data graphics must be readable: each graph runs >=5 s when the speech allows, reveals one element per spoken beat (>=0.5 s each), zooms in slowly (>=1 s) and holds the key point >=1.5 s.
- [R-every-graph-states-one-plain-takeaway-in] (candidate, 0.8, global) Every graph states one plain takeaway in big text (>=40 px) and uses labels >=32 px; concrete, meaningful encodings beat abstract decoration. Never invent statistics: use qualitative labels unless the user gives numbers.
- [R-factual-claims-in-graphics-are-checked] (candidate, 0.8, global) Factual claims in graphics (statistics, legal, financial, scientific) are checked against what the presenter actually said and against both directions of a risk (e.g. too early AND too late); when unsure, ask the presenter.
- [R-low-res-source-footage-1080-wide-offer-a] (candidate, 0.75, global) Low-res source footage (<1080 wide): offer an AI video upscale (with cost) before building.
- [R-captions-muted] (default, 0.7, global) Add word-synced captions for any speech (most viewers watch muted); highlight at most 1 word in 10.
- [R-music-bed] (default, 0.65, profile:talking_head_edu) Add a low music bed (-18 to -24 dB under voice) with ducking during speech.
- [R-hook-text-frame0] (default, 0.6, global) Show a 4-7 word text hook from frame 0 in the hook band; it complements the spoken line.
- [R-first-change-by-3s] (default, 0.6, global) First visual change (punch-in, cut, graphic) by ~2.5-3 s.
- [R-motivated-motion] (default, 0.6, global) Tie punch-ins and SFX to claims, punchlines and section changes; cap repeats (caps). Over-editing becomes its own pattern.
- [R-safe-zones] (default, 0.6, platform:instagram_reels) Keep all text and key graphics inside the platform safe zone; captions in caption_band; nothing under the right rail.
- [R-save-send-cta] (default, 0.6, platform:instagram_reels) End with a save or send CTA in the last 2-4 s; loop back to the hook when natural.
- [R-ramses-ending-signoff] (candidate, 0.6, brand:ramses) Sin CTA de seguir/guardar: el video termina en su despedida hablada ('Dios te bendiga'), como cierre escrito a mano
- [R-ramses-captions-chin] (candidate, 0.6, brand:ramses) Subtítulos siempre, justo bajo la barbilla (su cara llena la zona segura en selfies), 1-3 palabras, estilo editorial: serif con la palabra clave en itálica
- [R-ramses-references] (candidate, 0.6, brand:ramses) Referencias de edición: @iamsarahsnow (etiquetas de papel, texto serif mixto arriba de la cabeza), @its.sammywu (páginas recortadas sobre el torso), @joshguvi (tipografía cinética, firma escrita a mano), @artzenmedia (video que se encoge a tarjeta con esquinas redondas)
- [R-change-cadence] (default, 0.55, global) Never leave the frame visually static longer than profile.max_static_s; densest changes in the first 15 s.
- [R-light-leak-limit] (default, 0.55, global) Light leaks/flashes at most 1-2 per minute; hard cuts are the default transition.
